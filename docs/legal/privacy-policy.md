# Privacy Policy — MTGCardBelcher

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

This policy covers the **MTGCardBelcher** Reddit app, operated by u/Watchful1.

**What it reads.** In subreddits where it is installed, the app reads each new comment and post: its text, to find `[[card name]]` calls; its author, to avoid replying to other bots; and its post's title, to skip threads the subreddit has excluded.

**What it keeps.** The ids of the comments and posts it has replied to, for one day, so that it never replies to the same one twice. The app's log records the id of each comment or post it answers, with the card names in the call. Reddit keeps that log as part of its developer platform.

**Who it shares with.** The card names in a call are sent to [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) to look up the real card.

Uninstalling the app deletes what it keeps in that subreddit.
