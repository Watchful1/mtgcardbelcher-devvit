# Privacy Policy — MTGCardBelcher

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

This policy covers the **MTGCardBelcher** Reddit app, operated by u/Watchful1.

**What it reads.** In subreddits where it is installed, the app reads the text
and author of each new comment and post, to find `[[card name]]` calls and to
avoid replying to other bots.

**What it keeps.** The ids of the comments and posts it has replied to, for one
day, so that it never replies to the same one twice.

**Who it shares with.** The card names in a call are sent to
[Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) to look up the real card.

Contact u/Watchful1 on Reddit.
