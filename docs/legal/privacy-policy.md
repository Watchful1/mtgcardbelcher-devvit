# Privacy Policy — MTGCardBelcher

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

This policy covers the **MTGCardBelcher** Reddit app, operated by u/Watchful1.

**What it reads.** In subreddits where it is installed, the app reads each new comment and post: its text, to find `[[card name]]` calls; its author, to avoid replying to other bots; and its post's title, to skip weekly unjerk and bottom-scoring threads. It also reads the newest 1,000 posts on r/MTGCardBelcher, to find its images.

**What it keeps.** The ids of the comments and posts it has replied to, for one day, so that it never replies to the same one twice. It also keeps links to the joke images, cooldowns, collector numbers and any Reddit rate limit in force. The app's log records the id of each comment or post it answers, with the card names in the call, and the ids of its replies, of items it skipped, and of posts whose flair it changed. Reddit keeps that log as part of its developer platform.

**Who it shares with.** The card names in a call are sent to [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) to look up the real card.

Uninstalling the app deletes what it keeps in that subreddit.

Contact u/Watchful1 on Reddit.
