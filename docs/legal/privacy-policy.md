# Privacy Policy — MTGCardBelcher

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

**MTGCardBelcher** replies to `[[card name]]` calls in comments and posts with a
joke card image. It is operated by u/Watchful1.

It keeps the ids of the comments and posts it has replied to for a day, and a
list of the image submissions from r/MTGCardBelcher that it picks jokes from.

The card names in a call are sent to [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) to look up
the real card.

Contact u/Watchful1 on Reddit.
