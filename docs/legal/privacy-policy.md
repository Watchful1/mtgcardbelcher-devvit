# MTGCardBelcher Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

MTGCardBelcher is operated by the r/magicthecirclejerking mod team.

The app reads new comments and posts where it is installed, including their authors and titles, to find and answer `[[card name]]` calls. It also reads posts on r/MTGCardBelcher to find its images.

It keeps the ids of items it has answered for one day, plus image links, cooldowns, collector numbers and Reddit rate limits. Its log on Reddit's developer platform records item ids and the card names called. Uninstalling the app deletes what it keeps for that subreddit.

Card names in a call are sent to [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com) to look up the real card.

Contact: r/magicthecirclejerking modmail.
